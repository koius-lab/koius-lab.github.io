# Research Timeline

## Foundation Model Pretraining and Data Selection

KOIUS Lab began by exploring efficient approaches to training data selection under realistic computational constraints.

The initial motivation was to investigate whether CPU-efficient signals could identify useful examples without expensive per-example model scoring.

## Concept-Guided Pretraining

The research direction evolved toward concept-aware data selection.

Experiments explored:

- Concept extraction
- Inverted indexing
- Retrieval-based sampling
- BM25 ranking
- Curriculum masking

The goal was to understand whether structured knowledge signals could improve pretraining efficiency.

## Agent Memory Research

The lab is currently exploring memory systems for AI agents, focusing on how agents can store, retrieve, and update information over long horizons.
