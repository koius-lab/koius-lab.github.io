# Research

## Efficient Foundation Model Pretraining

KOIUS Lab studies methods for improving foundation model training efficiency through better organization and selection of training data.

The research investigates whether informative signals, concepts, and retrieval mechanisms can guide pretraining beyond uniform data sampling.

## Concept-Aware Data Selection

Current directions include:

- Concept indexing
- Retrieval-based training example selection
- BM25-based ranking
- Curriculum-aware masking
- Long-tail concept analysis

## Agent Memory Systems

The lab explores memory mechanisms for AI agents that operate over extended interactions.

Research areas include:

- Episodic memory
- Semantic memory
- Retrieval and consolidation
- Learnable memory management

## Scientific Approach

The lab focuses on measurable improvements through controlled experiments, reproducible evaluation, and careful analysis of model behavior.
