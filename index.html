KOIUS Lab

Knowledge-Oriented Intelligence Systems Lab

KOIUS Lab is a graduate research initiative exploring efficient and reliable artificial intelligence systems, with a focus on foundation model pretraining, adaptive data selection, and memory systems for AI agents.

Research

  • Efficient Foundation Model Pretraining
  • Data Selection and Curriculum Learning
  • Long-Term Memory Systems for AI Agents
  • Evaluation of Intelligent Systems
